🚀 What I'm Building

🚲 Camply — Campus Mobility & Errand Platform

Camply is a campus-focused mobility and errand-help product designed to make everyday tasks easier for university students.

The idea brings essential campus activities together in one platform.

Planned capabilities:

- 🛵 Campus ride requests.
- 🛍️ Errand requests and item purchasing.
- 📦 Local delivery coordination.
- 🎓 A campus-focused user experience.

Vision: One app, every campus task.

🎓 StudentPal — Academic Resource Platform

StudentPal is an academic resource platform designed for the Faculty of Computing at Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko (AAUA).

Its goal is to make academic resources and useful university information easier for students to access.

Key areas:

- 📚 Academic notes and learning resources.
- 📝 Assignments and academic announcements.
- 📅 Academic calendar and important dates.
- 🌍 Opportunities and student community.
- 🤖 Exploration of AI-powered resource discovery.

⚙️ ServeFlow — Restaurant Ordering API

ServeFlow explores how API-driven software can improve restaurant ordering and service workflows.

The goal is to help restaurants manage orders more efficiently through a structured backend system.

Areas of focus:

- 🍽️ Digital menu and order management.
- 🔌 API-first architecture.
- 🏪 Restaurant-specific data and permissions.
- 📋 Order status and workflow management.
- 💳 Payment status tracking and business logic.
- 📈 A foundation for a potential subscription-based software product.

🌍 Project Akungba Explorer

An exploration of how digital mapping and interactive geographic experiences can help people understand and navigate Akungba-Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria.

The broader vision includes exploring geographic information, interactive maps, and immersive 3D experiences.
